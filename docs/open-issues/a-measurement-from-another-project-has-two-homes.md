# A measurement taken in another project that a head leans on has two homes in the knowledge table

## Summary

This repository's root `CLAUDE.md` sends "a finding established outside this project that a
decision here leans on" to `docs/grounding/<subject>.md` in the Component the decision belongs to.
The primer routes a measurement "by what it serves: the head of the decision it supports". A head
that cites a measurement taken in thaum fits both rows. Which home is meant?

## Details

### What

- The root's row, in `instructions@where-knowledge-goes`: "a finding established outside this
  project that a decision here leans on | `docs/grounding/<subject>.md` in the Component the
  decision belongs to".
- `primer@where-knowledge-goes`: "**A measurement is routed by what it serves**: the head of the
  decision it supports, the issue entry it characterises, the tripwire whose threshold it is, or
  else the commit message that took it."
- `instructions@verify-before-relying`: "A figure in the core's documents measured in another
  project, thaum, names thaum as its source."
- `design@agent-skills@application-judged-by-diff` states counts measured in a pass of the
  design-record axis in thaum. No grounding directory exists under the docs of any Component, and the
  design homes cite thaum's measurements in several heads.

Found by the routing axis of the review of the intake of seven retrospective files, which did not
report it as a finding: the primer's row and the root's line on thaum's figures place such a figure
where it is.

### Why it matters

`primer@where-knowledge-goes` gives every durable statement one home. A writer who meets two rows
for one figure picks one by habit, and the next writer may pick the other, against
`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`.

### What would close it

The owner states the scope of the root's grounding row: for instance, a finding read in another
project's source or documentation, such as a specification, and not a figure measured in a session
of another project, which the head it supports keeps. The row is reworded to that scope.
