# The head on this repository's skill prefix also holds a decision about the published workflow

## Summary

`design@knowledge-architect@klarch-prefix` holds two decisions: this repository's own skills and
agents take the prefix `klarch-`, and the installed skills do not mention the collision. The second
is about what the installed text tells every adopting project, which the root CLAUDE.md routes to
the agent-skills design home.

## Details

### What

The head's last two sentences: "The installed skills do not mention the case: outside this
repository the collision is unlikely, and a rule for it in the shipped text would be read by every
installing project for a risk the owner judges very small." The root CLAUDE.md's knowledge table
gives a decision about the published workflow, "what the installed skills, agents and primer tell
every adopting project", the agent-skills design home as its home. The two parts lose to different
nearest rivals: a prefix other than `klarch-`, and a sentence on the collision in the shipped text.

Found by a read-only audit of the root's design heads, re-taken by reading the head against the
root CLAUDE.md's table and `design@agent-skills@one-decision-per-head`.

### Why it matters

A session changing what the installed skills say about the prefix searches the agent-skills design
home and does not find the decision, which strains `design@agent-skills@one-decision-per-head`;
a reversal of either part leaves the other in a head whose title no longer states it.

### What would close it

The second part moved into a head of the agent-skills design home, with its argument and the
owner's judgement quoted from its source, and `design@knowledge-architect@klarch-prefix` kept to the
prefix; or the next change that edits the head doing so, under
`design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch`.
