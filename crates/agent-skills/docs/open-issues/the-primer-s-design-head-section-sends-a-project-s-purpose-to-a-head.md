# The primer's section on design heads says a project's purpose belongs in a head, and its knowledge table sends it to the goals home

## Summary

`primer@design-heads`, under "What a head holds", says: "**A head carries intent, shape and the
standing argument, not implementation.** What the project is for, and how it is arranged in order
to get there, belongs here." The knowledge table of `primer@where-knowledge-goes` routes "what the
project, or one Component, is for, and what would show it achieved" to the Component's goals home.
Where does a statement of what the project is for go?

## Details

### What

Two readings of the head sentence: a head records the purpose a decision serves, citing the goal it
derives from; or a head is a home for the project's purpose itself, which the knowledge table gives
the goals home. A census agent of step 4 of the work on what enters agent-facing text reported
the pair. Not established: which reading the sentence was written for.

### Why it matters

`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-get-a-complete-workflow` asks that each instruction say what it
asks "plainly enough to follow without guessing what it means", and
`design@agent-skills@goals-change-through-goal-setting` keeps the purpose under the owner's word. A
session that writes a purpose into a head records an intent no goal-setting ruled on.

### What would close it

A reading of `design@agent-skills@one-home-for-head-rules` and of the goals' heads that states which
reading is meant, then a rewording of the primer's sentence to it.
