# The issue-tracking skill gives the cold-reader standard to `defect` alone in one place and to every entry in another

## Summary

`skill@knowledge-architect-issue-tracking@issue-entry` gives `defect` "the **full cold-reader
standard** below" and says "Only `defect` carries a mandatory checklist";
`skill@knowledge-architect-issue-tracking@cold-reader-standard` says "It binds every issue entry and
the commit message of every fix". Does a `question` or a `todo` entry owe the standard?

## Details

### What

Two readings: every entry owes the standard's items "as far as they exist", and `defect` owes all of
them as a checklist; or only `defect` owes the standard. A census agent of step 4 of the work on
what enters agent-facing text reported the three sentences. Not established: which reading is meant.

### Why it matters

`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-get-a-complete-workflow`: a session writing a `question` entry
cannot tell whether it owes the function names and the rulings-out of the standard, and a review can
report an entry for lacking them or not.

### What would close it

A reading of the heads on the issue register that states which kinds owe the standard, then a
rewording of the two sections to one reading.
