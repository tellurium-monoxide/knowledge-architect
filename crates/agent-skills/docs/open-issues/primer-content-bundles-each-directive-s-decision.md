# The head on the primer's content restates the primer's limit and bundles the decision behind each directive

## Summary

`design@agent-skills@primer-content` states, in its title, the rule that
`design@agent-skills@primer-limit` already holds, and its body carries the decision behind each of
the primer's directives, each with a nearest rival of its own. Under
`design@agent-skills@one-decision-per-head` that is one decision in two heads, and several decisions
in one.

## Details

### What

**The instance.** The title of `design@agent-skills@primer-content` reads "The primer carries the
directives that every session applies and that no skill delivers at the moment they apply", and the
title of `design@agent-skills@primer-limit` reads "The primer holds only what every session needs
and no installed skill delivers". Both answer what the primer holds, and lose to the same rival, a
primer that carries what a skill delivers. The body of `design@agent-skills@primer-content` then
argues each directive: the goals rule, the owner's word bound by its premise, the room to judge, the
intent-and-claims rule, the check before diagnosing, and the four outcomes of something met outside
the task. At least one of them lost to a rival of its own, recorded in
`path@agent-skills@docs/rejected-alternatives.md` as "Reporting a finding met outside the task
without recording it".

**Suspected mechanism**: the head listed its members in its title, and moving the title to its rule
made it the rule another head already states, while the members' own decisions stayed bundled.

**The re-entry point**: the next change that edits `design@agent-skills@primer-content`, which
`design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch` brings to the rules on heads; or a design discussion
under `skill@knowledge-architect-design` on the owner's word.

### Why it matters

A session that reverses or extends one directive cannot cite the decision it changes, since the
head names six, and the rule of the primer's content has two homes that can drift, against
`goal@knowledge-architect@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`.

### What would close it

The rule left to `design@agent-skills@primer-limit`, whose body names the primer's members, and one
head for each directive whose decision has a rival of its own, the references to
`design@agent-skills@primer-content` in the root CLAUDE.md, the issue entries and the rejected
alternatives re-pointed; or the owner's ruling that the head stays as it is,
recorded in this entry's closing commit.
