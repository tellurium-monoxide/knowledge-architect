# A criterion derived from a decision binds until the owner says otherwise

## Summary

The design skill keeps a criterion derived from a recorded decision binding "until the owner says
otherwise". The primer lets the session reverse a head whose argument it finds defeated, and sends
the reversal to the owner only at a fork or where the head's ground is the owner's. In a discussion
the two disagree on who can lift such a criterion.

## Details

### What

`skill@knowledge-architect-design@threads-and-states`, on criteria, reads: "A criterion derived from a
recorded decision is binding as a stated presumption until the owner says otherwise, because a
decision binds only as a presumption that a better argument may rebut, per `primer@goals-bind`."
The only moves it gives are to change the proposal or for the owner to demote the criterion.

`primer@intent-and-claims` says: "Where the session finds the head's argument defeated, the head's
reversal goes to `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, and to the owner where reversing it is a fork
or the head's ground is the owner's". `primer@question-authority` gives the session what follows
from the record.

So a discussion that shows a recorded decision's argument defeated, with no fork and no ground of
the owner's, may still not lift the criterion derived from it without the owner's word. The
sentence predates the authority routing; a slice 4 review of the milestone that wrote that routing
found it.

### Why it matters

`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift` asks for an owner ruling only on what the
goals and the record leave open. `design@agent-skills@questions-go-to-their-authority` makes the
primer section prevail over an installed instruction that sends to the owner a question it gives
the session, so the skill's sentence is an instruction a session is told to override, against
`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-get-a-complete-workflow`, which asks that each instruction be
plain enough to follow without guessing what it means.

### What would close it

The sentence rewritten so that a criterion derived from a decision binds until the decision is
reversed, by the session where its argument is defeated and no fork or owner's ground stands, or
by the owner otherwise. The rewrite passes the standard that `path@agent-skills@CLAUDE.md` names under "Editing
an installed skill or agent".
