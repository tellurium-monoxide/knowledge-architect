# The planning skill forbids "plan" alone as a document's name and uses it alone

## Summary

`skill@knowledge-architect-planning@planning-terms` says: "The word "plan" alone never names a
document: say spec, milestone document, or plan document." The same skill writes "the plan is a
spec's id", "inside its own plan", "the plan is cited whole" and "a commit message cites a plan only
whole". Is "plan" there the name of a document, or of an item's anchor?

## Details

### What

Two readings of the later uses: "plan" names the plan document, against the rule; or it names the
anchor a plan document gives its items, a different thing the rule does not cover. A census agent
of step 4 of the work on what enters agent-facing text reported the conflict. Not established: which
reading the rule allows.

### Why it matters

`design@agent-skills@document-vocabulary` holds the plan documents' words to "one word to one
meaning"; a session following the rule cannot tell whether those sentences break it, and a reviewer
can report either.

### What would close it

A reading of `design@agent-skills@document-vocabulary` that states whether the anchor sense is a
second meaning, then a rewording of the rule or of the uses.
