# The design skill's duty to bring back a material finding has no design head

## Summary

The design skill states, in `skill@knowledge-architect-design@decision-authority`, that a decision is never assumed to have seen
all its consequences, and that a material finding comes back to the owner exactly once, with a
default. The planning skill restates it. No head of the agent-skills design home records that
decision, so a head that argues from it can describe it and cannot cite it.

## Details

### What

The duty is in `skill@knowledge-architect-design@decision-authority`:
"**Never assume the decision saw all its consequences.**" and "**Material findings come back
exactly once.**", where a finding is material when it defeats "a reason the ruling gave, a premise
it rested on, or a criterion it claimed to meet". `path@agent-skills@content/skills/planning/SKILL.md`
restates it in its vocabulary table, row **material**.

A search of the design home's headings for `owner`, `ruling`, `word`, `closed`, `material`,
`authority` and `reopen` returns no head that states the duty. The new bullet of
`design@agent-skills@primer-content` on a word of the owner and its premise argues that the design
skill "holds the same duty for a closed thread", and names it in words only. A decision-record
review of that change found the gap. The duty is stated at two sites, so test 2 of
`design@agent-skills@a-head-is-owed-by-an-entry-test` is expected to admit it; not established
until the head is written and the tests are applied.

### Why it matters

A reversal or a widening of the material-finding duty would leave
`design@agent-skills@primer-content` arguing from a duty that changed, and `cargo klarch show` would
not list that head among what the change touches, against
`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`.

### What would close it

A head in `path@agent-skills@docs/design.md` recording the duty and its argument, written under
`knowledge-architect-decision-recording` and argued under `knowledge-architect-design` since it
creates a head, and the bullet of `design@agent-skills@primer-content` citing it. Or the owner's
ruling that the duty earns no head, recorded in this entry's closing commit.
