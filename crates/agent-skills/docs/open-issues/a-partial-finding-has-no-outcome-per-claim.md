# The review skill does not ask for an outcome for each claim of a finding

## Summary

A reviewer's finding often holds several claims. The review skill says findings become repairs or
issues, and says nothing of a finding acted on in part. Finding W4 of the retrospective of
2026-10-04.

## Details

### What

In the session of that retrospective, two findings were acted on in part, in the commit "[review]
Five reviewers read the pre-release fixes: the setup skill's local-install branch and the bump
table's manifest row are repaired". The decision-record
reviewer's finding on a narrowed head also said that the reversal owed a report of the search for
the incumbent; the self-consistency reviewer's finding on the same edit also said that it weakened
a goal. The heads were repaired, and the other two claims had no outcome until the transcript
reviewer listed them.

### Why it matters

A claim left without an outcome is lost unless the transcript review finds it. The goal claim was
a decision-against-goal conflict, which the primer sends to the owner, against
`goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides`.

### What would close it

A line in `knowledge-architect-review`, section 3: each claim of a finding gets its own outcome,
repaired, opened as an issue, or judged to need nothing with the reason. Or the owner's ruling that
the transcript review is the intended catch.
