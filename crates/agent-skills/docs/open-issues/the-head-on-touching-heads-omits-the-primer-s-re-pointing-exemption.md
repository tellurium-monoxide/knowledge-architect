# The head on touching a head omits the primer's exemption for a re-pointed reference

## Summary

`design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch` says that a head a change edits is brought, in that
change, to the rules on heads, and names no exemption. The installed primer, `primer@design-heads`,
states the same rule and adds that re-pointing a reference in a head does not touch it. The head and
the installed text it decides disagree on whether a change that only re-points a reference owes the
head's repairs. Which of the two is the defect is not established.

## Details

### What

- The head, in the agent-skills design home: "A head that a change edits is brought, in that
  change, to the rules of `primer@design-heads`: its title to the rule it argues, each decision it
  bundles to a head of its own, its ground to its argument." The paragraphs after it name no
  exemption.
- The primer, in its section on how a head is shaped: "A head that a change touches is brought to
  these rules in that change [...] Re-pointing a reference in a head does not touch it."
- Found by the self-consistency reviewer of the review of the register-references spec, and
  confirmed by the transcript reviewer of the same review. The work of that spec, which built
  `design@core@path-to-a-home-is-reported`, relied on the primer's exemption: its migration
  re-pointed a reference inside `design@core@candidate-rule-and-retired-forms`, and triggered none
  of the splits that `issue@core@four-heads-bundle-decisions-with-many-citing-sites` owes at the
  next edit of that head.

### Why it matters

`design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch` is what a session reads to decide whether its change
owes a head's repairs. Under the head alone, any change that re-points a reference in a bundled head
owes its split, as a mechanical respelling or a rename does across many heads at once; under the
primer it owes nothing. A session that follows one and a reviewer that follows the other disagree on
whether the change is complete, against `goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`.

### What would close it

The head states the exemption, with its reason, if the primer's sentence is the intended rule; or
the primer drops the sentence, if the head's wording is. Either change is one commit that edits the
other text to agree, and the re-entry point of
`issue@core@four-heads-bundle-decisions-with-many-citing-sites` is read again against the result.
