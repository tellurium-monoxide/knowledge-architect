# The planning skill answers every material finding with a default, and states no hold for one that defeats a premise of the owner's ruling

## Summary

`skill@knowledge-architect-planning@working-a-slice` and the planning skill's vocabulary row
**material** answer a material finding with a default. The design skill and the primer hold the part
of the work that rests on a finding that defeats a premise of a ruling of the owner, or is a
contradiction no argument resolves. Does the planning skill's sentence send a session past that
hold?

## Details

### What

- `path@agent-skills@content/skills/planning/SKILL.md`, in the paragraph on what the plan
  reviewers' findings become: "A material finding is answered with a default, written into the
  sections it touches." Its vocabulary row **material** says a material finding "is presented once,
  with a default named", and names no hold.
- `skill@knowledge-architect-design@decision-authority`: "When the finding defeats a premise of a
  ruling the owner gave, or is a contradiction with another decided thread that no argument
  resolves, it is a contradiction, per `primer@owner-word-premise`: the part of the work that rests
  on it is held until the word comes, and the rest proceeds on the default."
- `primer@owner-word-premise`: "A ruling on intent whose premise proves false is a contradiction.
  … the part of the work that rests on it waits for the answer, and the rest goes on."
- The instance: D20 of this repository's owner-authority milestone, a material finding against
  the approved thread that `design@agent-skills@attribution-replaces-quotation` records, was listed
  as a default awaiting the owner, and its slice merged with it, ruled at the next slice's start.
  W4 of that milestone's retrospective reported the session's confusion over defaults; the session
  of the intake of seven retrospective files found the planning sentence while answering it.
- `design@agent-skills@default-ruled-before-merge` keeps a default unruled from merging, so the
  merge of that instance is closed. Whether the part of a slice's work that rests on a contradiction
  is held before the merge, as the primer asks, is not.

Not established: whether a session that follows the planning skill reads the primer's hold as
prevailing over the planning sentence. The owner judged, in the discussion of W4, that the current
routing is what the owner wants, on the reading that a question with no sensible default holds its
part, which the primer and the design skill already state.

### Why it matters

Two installed instructions that answer one moment differently leave a session to pick one, against
`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`. Work built on a defeated premise of the
owner's ruling is the case `goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-s-word-is-verified` guards.

### What would close it

Either the planning sentence and its vocabulary row point to
`skill@knowledge-architect-design@decision-authority` for which outcome a material finding takes,
default or hold, and stop restating one of the two; or a reading shows that a plan document's
material finding never rests work on a contradiction before the owner rules, and this entry closes
with that reading.
