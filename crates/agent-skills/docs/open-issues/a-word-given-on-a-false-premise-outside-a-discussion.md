# A word of the owner given on a premise that later proved false has no rule outside a design discussion

## Summary

The owner said heads "should be kept anyway" in the belief that they passed other entry tests; they
did not, and the agent acted on the word. The design skill already says a decision may rest on a
premise it did not check, but only a design discussion delivers it, and this word came during a
review repair. Where should the rule be delivered? Third case of finding W2 of
2026-10-07-knowledge-architect-workflow.

## Details

### What

The owner's words, after the fact, in the message of the commit "A decision-record review read test
4's new shape": "Me saying "They should be kept anyway" was me thinking that those heads would pass
other record tests, not a direct ruling."

The duty exists, in `path@agent-skills@content/skills/design/SKILL.md`, section Decision authority:
"**Never assume the decision saw all its consequences.**", and a material finding is one that
defeats "a reason the ruling gave, a premise it rested on, or a criterion it claimed to meet". A
session repairing a review finding is under no design skill, so the rule does not reach it. Test 4
of decision-recording now asks the owner whether a word records intent, which covers the first two
cases of the finding, an approval and a hedge, and not this one.

The primer holds no rule on the owner's words. The retrospective proposed "the primer's owner-word
rules" as a placement; no such section exists.

### Why it matters

A word acted on past its premise records a decision the owner did not make, against
`goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides`.

### What would close it

A ruling on where the rule is delivered, and the edit:

- beside test 4 of decision-recording, where this case arose: one site of a rule that is not
  specific to decision recording;
- or the primer, which reaches every session and is held to what every session needs and no skill
  delivers, per `design@agent-skills@primer-limit`.
