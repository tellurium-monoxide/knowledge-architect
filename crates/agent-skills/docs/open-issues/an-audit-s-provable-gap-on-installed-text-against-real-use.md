# An audit's provable gap on the installed text goes to the owner as an edit, while an addition to the installed text needs real use

## Summary

The agentic-workflow axis of the project-audit skill admits a provable gap as a finding, and its
outcome table puts a gap whose filling adds an instruction on the owner list. In this repository
an installed-text finding is then edited at its source. The design head on additions says a review
finding originates an edit only for a contradiction, a broken trigger or a factual error. Which of
the two governs an audit's provable gap on the installed text is not decided.

## Details

### What

The two texts:

- `skill@knowledge-architect-project-audit@agentic-workflow-axis`, its outcome table: "a provable
  gap, since filling it adds an instruction" is on the owner list; and the workflow auditor's lens
  L4, `agent@knowledge-architect-workflow-auditor@the-lenses`, drafts provable gaps.
- `design@agent-skills@additions-need-real-use`: "A review finding originates an edit only where
  its defect is provable by reading: a contradiction, a broken trigger, a factual error. A finding
  that predicts a behaviour is parked as an issue". Its test for an addition asks for a behaviour
  seen in a real session, the owner naming what was lacked, and a one-sentence mechanism.

`path@agent-skills@CLAUDE.md` restates the second for every edit under
`path@agent-skills@content/`.

Met at the fifth pass of the agentic-workflow audit, reported by its L2 walk of the audit and
retrospective activities. Two repairs of that pass filled provable gaps on installed text. On the
owner's ruling, both were parked as issue entries instead of edits. One, on an upstream file that
nothing fed from an earlier pass, closed when each pass came to commit a report file; the other is
`issue@agent-skills@the-transcript-reviewer-cannot-see-a-plan-document-its-range-deletes`.

### Why it matters

`design@agent-skills@additions-need-real-use` derives from
`goal@knowledge-architect@the-workflow-improves-through-real-use`. An audit that edits installed
text on a provable gap alone grows the installed text by the route that head closes. An audit that
parks every such gap leaves the L4 lens with no outcome but an issue, which the audit skill does
not say.

### What would close it

A ruling of the owner, under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, on whether a provable gap found by
reading counts as provable by reading for the head. Then either the head names it beside the three
classes, or the audit's outcome table sends a provable gap on the installed text to an issue entry.
