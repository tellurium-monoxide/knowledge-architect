# A pending retrospective analysis is outstanding work that the searches at a design's grounding and at a design audit do not read

## Summary

An analysis in `path@knowledge-architect@docs/retrospective-reports/` holds outcomes the owner
ruled "handle now" and that no commit has carried out yet. The root CLAUDE.md delivers
`ls docs/retrospective-reports/` at one moment: before diagnosing a problem. The searches of
outstanding work at a design discussion's grounding and at the design audit of a slice or a spec
read the issue and tripwire registers only. A design could then be built past a ruled outcome. This
is a prediction: no real session has shown it.

## Details

### What

Texts read, at the commit the third pass of the first agentic-workflow audit ran on:

- `instructions@verify-before-relying`, its section "Check whether it is already known": the
  `ls docs/retrospective-reports/` line, under "Before diagnosing anything as a problem".
- `skill@klarch-retrospective-intake@after-the-ruling`: "Until it leaves, an analysis is
  outstanding work that no register lists."
- `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, the grounding, and
  `skill@knowledge-architect-planning@working-a-slice` point 2, the design audit: both dispatch
  `agent@knowledge-architect-standing-entry-searcher`, which reads `cargo klarch issues` and
  `cargo klarch tripwires` only.

The installed searches cannot name this repository's directory, since installed text names no
convention of one project. Reported by the L6 lens of the third pass, as a predicted gap.

Two repairs were considered and not made, since each adds an instruction on a prediction:

- the root names the grounding and the design audit as moments of the `ls` too;
- every "handle now" outcome also opens an issue entry, so the registers hold it; the intake rules
  that such an outcome "needs no issue entry", so as not to keep the same work in two homes.

### Why it matters

`design@knowledge-architect@committed-findings-analysis` keeps an analysis until its last outcome is
carried out, on the premise that the work it records is found while it waits. A design that
contradicts or duplicates a ruled outcome costs the work twice.

### What would close it

A retrospective of a session in this repository that designed or landed work bearing on a pending
analysis: either the session found the analysis, and the entry closes, or it did not, and the root
names the grounding and the design audit as moments of the search.
